## BLE RC Controller v1.0.2

**English** — First version tried on a real Android tablet with two cars. Fixes the key names, adds on-screen toggles and animations.
**Español** — Primera versión probada en una tableta Android real con dos coches. Corrige los nombres de teclas y añade interruptores en pantalla y animaciones.
**Português** — Primeira versão testada em um tablet Android de verdade com dois carrinhos. Corrige os nomes das teclas e adiciona botões liga/desliga na tela e animações.

### Fixed
- **Keys were shown as numbers** (for example `458834`) in the **Buttons** tab of the 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 release builds, on every system. They now read ↑ W S Space.
- ◀ ▶ were drawn as orange emoji on Android; they are icons now.
- The on-screen buttons sit at the bottom of the screen, where the thumbs rest, instead of at the top.
- The app no longer fails when Android's "Remove animations" setting is on.
- The **Disconnect** button and some texts no longer overflow on narrow screens.
- **Disconnect now stays disconnected.** Before, the app reconnected on its own to the car that was still switched on. A connection that drops by itself still reconnects.

### Changed
- On the on-screen buttons, **Turbo** and **360° spin** now switch on and off with each tap, like the light. With a controller or keyboard they still apply only while held. They switch off by themselves when you disconnect, lose the connection or leave the app.
- The **Mode 1/2** button is gone. It had no visible effect on either car tested, so the app always sends mode 1. See `docs/PROTOCOL.md`.

### New
- Animations: smooth tab changes, cars sliding into the list, a car icon that sways while driving and leans in turns, a battery icon that follows the level, and touch buttons that press in. They follow the system "reduce motion" setting.

### Tested
- **Android:** a Lenovo tablet (Android 16), two cars (SF-25 and 296 GT3), a DualShock 4 and the on-screen buttons. Driving, steering, light, turbo and 360° spin were checked on the cars.
- **Windows: not tested on a real PC yet.** The package comes from CI, which builds it and runs the automated tests. Reports are welcome.
- **macOS:** verified earlier with one car and a DualShock 4; this build has not been run on a car.
- 65 automated tests.
