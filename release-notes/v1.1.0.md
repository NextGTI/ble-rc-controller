## BLE RC Controller v1.1.0

**English** — The whole app is redesigned: a dark look with one amber accent, a dashboard that opens first, and clear screens for every connection problem. Same controls, new navigation.
**Español** — Toda la app se ha rediseñado: aspecto oscuro con un solo acento ámbar, un panel que se abre primero y pantallas claras para cada problema de conexión. Mismos controles, navegación nueva.
**Português** — O app inteiro foi redesenhado: visual escuro com um único destaque âmbar, um painel que abre primeiro e telas claras para cada problema de conexão. Mesmos controles, navegação nova.

### New
- **New look:** dark, with a single amber accent and the Saira typeface. The dashboard draws the car from above with a steering gauge and a throttle scale, and dust particles react to the car. The state cells (light, turbo, spin, controller) are frosted glass.
- **Dashboard first, no tabs.**
  - **Phone and tablet:** tap the car name to open the car sheet, where you pick, switch or disconnect a car and set **Reconnect to the last car**. The gear opens **Settings**.
  - **Computer:** a garage column on the left lists the cars and controllers, a key legend sits under the dashboard, and **Settings** opens as a panel on the right. Esc or a click outside closes it.
- **Settings** now holds **Buttons and keys**, **Controllers** (live signal meters and **Teach a new controller**), **On-screen buttons**, **Stick dead zone**, **Reconnect to the last car**, **Language** and **About**.
- **Clear warning screens** for: searching, Bluetooth off (with a **Turn on Bluetooth** button on Android), Bluetooth blocked, Bluetooth LE not available, connecting (with **Cancel**), connection lost, and could not connect. When the connection is lost the app reconnects by itself, and tries again if an attempt fails.
- **Teach wizard redrawn:** a drawing of the controller lights the part to press, 18 steps in 5 groups, and each step names the button on other brands ("✕ on PlayStation, A on Xbox, B on Switch").
- New app icon. Dark system bars on Android.

### Changed
- The Cars, Buttons and Controllers tabs are gone. Cars are in the car sheet (phone, tablet) or the garage column (computer); the rest is in Settings.
- **Input monitor** is now **Live signals**, inside Controllers.
- **Settings → Show on-screen controls** is now **Settings → On-screen buttons**.

### Fixed
- Leaving **Buttons and keys** while it was waiting for an input no longer freezes driving.
- A failed reconnect is tried again instead of giving up.
- A released key always stops the car.
- Phones in landscape get a two-column layout.
- The garage column keeps clear of the system bars.

### Tested
- **Android:** a Lenovo tablet (Android 16), two cars (SF-25 and 296 GT3) and a DualShock 4. Driving, losing and regaining the car, turning Bluetooth off, switching cars, Settings and teaching the controller were checked by hand.
- **macOS:** tested with the cars and a DualShock 4.
- **Windows:** tested on a PC with the cars and a DualShock 4.
- 1065 automated tests.
